Vendored FFmpeg libraries
========================

Source: https://github.com/libretro-fork/ppsspp-ffmpeg
Revision: 6c64b8f98ad0ca38f5360484618e2af6e44fcb3d

The core builds libavcodec, libavformat, libavutil, libswresample and libswscale
using ext/ffmpeg-build. These library trees and compat are copied unchanged
from the revision above. Prebuilt archives, standalone applications and their
build tooling are omitted. Licensing information and license texts are retained.
FFmpeg is tracked directly in the parent repository; no FFmpeg submodule is required.
